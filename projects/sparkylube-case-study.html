Case study

SparkyLube.

Client website · Brantford, ON · Mar – Jul 2025

4,300+ unique visitors, 344 confirmed clients upsold in the 2025 campaign.

Situation

An oil-change shop needed a website its customers could actually use. After a bad experience with a previous provider, the owners needed a site they could trust — one that worked for customers, not against them.

Task

Print-friendly service menus, integrated online booking, and local SEO — a site that turns searches into bookings.

Action

Built in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript through MOLEX, with the internship work done alongside Alex. Service menus customers can print, booking built in, and local search set up so the shop shows up where its customers are looking.

Result

4,300+ unique visitors and 344 confirmed clients upsold in the 2025 campaign. A demo of the finished site is preserved on this portfolio.
