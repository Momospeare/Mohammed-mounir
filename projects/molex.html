Case study

MOLEX Digital Solutions.

Co-founder · Feb 2025 – Present

Two client sites live: SparkyLube — 4,300+ unique visitors, 344 confirmed clients upsold in 2025; True Shield Security — official site launched 2026, now multiple clients and hiring.

Situation

Small businesses around him needed websites that bring in customers, not just look good. He co-founded MOLEX with Alex in February 2025 to build them — websites, lead generation, and the digital presence around them.

Task

Turn a small business's online presence into clients and revenue: a site people can use, find, and book through — and a way to tell whether it's working.

Action

Every build runs the same way: intake, build, QA, launch, then Revision Care. Builds run on Webflow; Facebook Lead Ads and Google Business Profile bring the leads in; GA4 tracking shows what's actually working. The MOLEX site itself is the inquiry and consult platform.

Result

SparkyLube's site did 4,300+ unique visitors and 344 confirmed clients upsold in the 2025 campaign. True Shield Security's official site launched in 2026 and the company now has multiple clients and is hiring. Both clients stayed — True Shield is a long-term relationship, not a one-off build, and MOLEX remains their digital solutions platform.
