03 How a build runs

Intake, build, QA, launch, Revision Care.

Every build runs the same way. Each site is built to bring in customers — a site people can use, find, and book through — with lead capture and tracking that shows what's actually working. The MOLEX site itself is the inquiry and consult platform.

Intake

What the business does, who its customers are, and what the site needs to do for them.

Build

The site, built around the business — pages, booking, and lead capture included.

QA

Checked before launch — links, forms, booking, and tracking all working.

Launch

Live, indexed, and handed over — with the business able to reach its customers from day one.

Revision Care

Ongoing updates and fixes after launch, so the site keeps working as the business grows.
