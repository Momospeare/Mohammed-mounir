True Shield Security.

Official site · Launched 2026

Official site launched 2026 — the company now has multiple clients and is hiring.

Situation

True Shield Security, a security company run by Anwar Bonimy, needed an official site that matched the work — and a way for clients to reach them.

Task

Security-industry positioning, service pages, and an inquiry flow that turns visits into calls.

Action

Built through MOLEX and launched in 2026. Anwar is also the host of the Shielded Truth podcast with Mohammed — the site and the podcast carry the same law-and-policing side of his work.

Result

The company now has multiple clients and is hiring. The relationship continued past launch: MOLEX remains True Shield's digital solutions platform — proof of loyalty and long-term work, not a one-off build.
