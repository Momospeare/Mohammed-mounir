About

Investigations first. Systems next.

I'm Mohammed Mounir. I work in asset protection at Loblaw, where I run investigations, build evidence packages, and keep stores ahead of organized retail crime. The long game is crime intelligence analysis, with auxiliary volunteering alongside it — the same instinct, applied differently: protect people, then build the systems that protect them.
