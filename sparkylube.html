SparkyLube

Company website for a Brantford, ON automotive / oil-change business. Designed, built, and maintained Mar – Jul 2025.

Demo reconstruction — the live client site is currently offline, so this page shows what MOLEX built and what it delivered, not a copy of the live site.

What was built

The site

Print-friendly service menus

Service menus customers can read on screen and print at the counter — no PDFs to maintain, no reprints.

Integrated online booking

Customers book their oil change online instead of phoning in — fewer missed calls, fewer no-shows.

Local SEO + Google Business Profile

Built to be found by people searching for an oil change in Brantford — listings, reviews, and local search.

GA4 data tracking

Every visit and booking measured, so the business knows which channels bring customers — and which don't.
